Grundriss

From a sketch to a house you can walk around.

Trace a scanned floor plan, draw and edit it in 2D, and see it in 3D at the same time – with stories, stairs, any roof, furniture, wiring, heating, energy, and solar.

Scroll to build the house ↓

01 Trace

Start with the paper you have

Load a scan or a photo of an old plan. A neural network finds the walls, the doors, and the windows right in your browser, and you accept or reject each suggestion. Calibrate one known length, and the plan has the right scale.

02 Draw

Walls that know their rooms

Draw walls with snapping and angles, drop in doors and windows, and move a wall – the next walls follow. Rooms close by themselves, with their names and areas.

03 3D

See it rise

Each line you draw is a wall in the 3D view next to it, with its thickness, its height, and holes for every door and window. Orbit around it while you edit.

04 Stories

Up the stairs

Add a cellar, more floors, or an attic. Straight, L, and U stairs climb to the next story, and chimneys go up through all of them.

05 Roof

Any roof, on any outline

Gable, hip, shed, or flat – or set each side on its own. A straight-skeleton engine builds the faces on any outline, with dormers and roof windows. The attic walls stop where the roof starts.

06 Furniture

Make it a home

Pick from simple pieces with free sizes, the Kenney Furniture Kit, realistic models from Poly Haven, or your own glTF files. A piece near a wall turns its back to it.

07 Services

Every socket, every pipe

Place sockets, switches, lights, radiators, and the heat pump, and connect them with cables and pipes. Grundriss counts the devices and measures every line.

08 Energy

Know the heat, catch the sun

Give the walls, the roof, and the floor their layers, and get the U-values and the heat loss of each room. Fill the roof with solar panels and see their yield.

09 Export

Take it anywhere

Print the plan to PDF at 1:50 or 1:100, open it in CAD as DXF, or take the house to Blender as glTF. One package file keeps everything: the plan, the scans, and the models.

Draw your house tonight.

Grundriss runs in your browser. There is nothing to install, no account, and no server: your plans never leave your computer.
